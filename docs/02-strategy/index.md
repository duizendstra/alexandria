# 02 — Strategy

This folder captures the long-term direction and growth plan for the Alexandria module ecosystem.

## What Belongs Here

- **Vision** — The overarching purpose and goals of the Alexandria project.
- **Roadmap** — Planned modules, milestones, and timelines.
- **Module Growth Strategy** — Criteria for when a package graduates from internal to public.
- **Publication Criteria** — Quality gates a module must pass before publishing to pkg.go.dev.

* [Capability Graduation & Roadmap Strategy](roadmap.md) - Defines the lifecycle, quality gates, and graduation roadmap for moving custom codebase utilities into standard, shared ecosystem capabilities.
