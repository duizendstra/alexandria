# 01 — Governance

This folder contains the foundational policies and community standards that govern the Alexandria project.

## What Belongs Here

- **Licensing** — License terms and third-party attribution notices.
- **Contributing Guidelines** — How to propose changes, branch conventions, and PR expectations.
- **Changelog** — A chronological record of releases and notable changes.
- **Release Policy** — Versioning scheme, release cadence, and stability guarantees.
- **Code of Conduct** — Community behavior standards and enforcement procedures.
